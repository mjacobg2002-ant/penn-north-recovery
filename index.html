Who We Are

More than treatment — a place to rebuild a life.

Penn North Recovery, a program of Maryland Community Health Initiatives, has served the Pennsylvania Avenue community for more than two decades — helping individuals and families overcome substance use, mental health challenges, poverty, and homelessness.

Our approach is trauma-informed and whole-person: we treat addiction and co-occurring mental health conditions together, then surround each person with the wellness, employment, and community support they need to stay well.

Everyone who walks through our doors is met with respect — no matter where they are in their journey.

What We Offer

Care that treats the whole person

From clinical addiction treatment to acupuncture, employment coaching, and community wellness — everything you need to heal, in one place.

Addiction Treatment

Evidence-based outpatient and intensive outpatient care for drug and alcohol use disorders.

  • Individual & group counseling
  • Cognitive behavioral therapy
  • Relapse prevention
  • MAT-friendly & NA meetings

Mental Health

Integrated care for co-occurring conditions, delivered with a trauma-informed lens.

  • Co-occurring disorder treatment
  • Trauma-informed therapy
  • Anger management
  • Motivational interviewing

Acudetox & Acupuncture

Auricular acupuncture in a supportive group setting to ease cravings and stress.

  • Weekly acupuncture
  • Oriental medicine
  • Mind-body healing circles
  • Yoga & meditation

Employment & Reentry

Practical support to help people return to the workforce with a living wage.

  • Resume & job coaching
  • GED & computer literacy
  • Federal bonding
  • Expungement guidance

Community & Wellness

Wraparound support that meets basic needs and builds lasting stability.

  • Food assistance (Maryland Food Bank)
  • Nutrition & cooking classes
  • Smoking cessation
  • HIV/STD testing

Peer & Family Support

Recovery is stronger together — with peers who've walked the path and families beside you.

  • Peer recovery specialists
  • Family engagement
  • Life skills training
  • Sober living support
Meeting You Where You Are

Levels of care that grow with you

Whether you need intensive daily support or flexible ongoing care, we have a program that fits your life.

LEVEL 01

Day Treatment (PHP)

Structured, intensive daytime programming for those who need the most support while living at home.

LEVEL 02

Intensive Outpatient

Several sessions a week of counseling and group therapy that flex around work and family.

LEVEL 03

Outpatient Treatment

Ongoing individual and group counseling to maintain and strengthen your recovery over time.

LEVEL 04

Sober Living Support

Guidance and connection to safe, supportive housing as you rebuild independence.

ALONGSIDE

Co-Occurring Care

Mental health and substance use treated together, never in isolation.

ALONGSIDE

Medication-Assisted

MAT-friendly: we support clients prescribed MAT and treat alcohol use disorder.
