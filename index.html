Who We Are

Treatment rooted in community.

Penn North Recovery is a program of Maryland Community Health Initiatives, providing integrated, community-based addiction treatment and wellness services in West Baltimore.

Our approach combines clinical treatment, mental-health support, employment services, and whole-person wellness — surrounding each person with the care they need to recover and stay well. Everyone who walks through our doors is treated with dignity and respect, wherever they are in their journey.
